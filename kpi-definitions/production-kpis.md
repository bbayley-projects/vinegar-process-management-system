# VPMS Production KPI Definitions

## Purpose

This document defines the principal performance indicators used in the portfolio reconstruction of the Vinegar Process Management System (VPMS).

The calculations use fictional demonstration data contained in the repository and are intended to demonstrate the analytical structure of the system.

## KPI Definitions

### 1. Total Planned Production

Total volume scheduled for production during the selected reporting period.

**Calculation**

Total Planned Production = Sum of Planned Volume (L)

---

### 2. Total Actual Production

Total volume actually produced during the selected reporting period.

**Calculation**

Total Actual Production = Sum of Actual Volume (L)

---

### 3. Production Attainment

Measures actual production relative to planned production.

**Calculation**

Production Attainment (%) =  
(Total Actual Production / Total Planned Production) × 100

This provides a high-level indicator of production performance against plan.

---

### 4. Total Downtime

Total recorded production downtime during the selected reporting period.

**Calculation**

Total Downtime = Sum of Downtime Minutes

This can also be analysed by date, product and issue category.

---

### 5. Average Downtime per Batch

Measures the average recorded downtime associated with each production batch.

**Calculation**

Average Downtime per Batch =  
Total Downtime Minutes / Number of Production Batches

---

### 6. Conforming Batch Rate

Measures the proportion of production batches recorded as conforming.

**Calculation**

Conforming Batch Rate (%) =  
(Conforming Batches / Total Batches) × 100

---

### 7. Batches Requiring Review

Count of production batches with a Quality Status of Review.

This provides visibility into batches requiring additional attention or follow-up.

---

### 8. Issue Frequency

Count of production batches associated with a recorded operational issue.

Issues may be analysed by category, including:

- Equipment
- Process
- Material

---

### 9. Distribution Completion Rate

Measures the proportion of production batches with completed distribution status.

**Calculation**

Distribution Completion Rate (%) =  
(Completed Distribution Records / Total Distribution Records) × 100

---

## Analytical Use

These KPIs support management views for:

- Production performance monitoring
- Plan-versus-actual analysis
- Downtime analysis
- Quality-status monitoring
- Operational issue analysis
- Distribution monitoring
- Trend identification
